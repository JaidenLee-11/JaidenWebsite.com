What is Water Polo?

Water polo is a competitive water sport between two teams of 7 players. The game has 4 quarters where the opposing team's attempt to shoot the ball into the other team's goal. Similar to something like soccer you constantly attempt to get the ball, swim to the otherside, and score.

Why Should You Join?

Water polo is a really enjoyable sport played in the pool. The small team allows for a good way to meet new people and be part of something. No experience is needed. In addition, it is free varsity along with being quite easy to learn and enjoy.

How to Join

To join simply go to the Home Campus website where you can fill out your information and select which sports you play for that year.
